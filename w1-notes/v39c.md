**Carried into #40** (low, all pre-existing or test gaps; none is an egress or private-address issue):
1. **A slow PR client can still be cut, with a clean FIN, while it keeps reading.** The upstream's idle timer counts time paused by backpressure as idleness. With `idleMs` 300 and one read every 100 ms, a 2 MiB body arrived about 1.2 MB short. Under the 5-minute default this takes a reader that drains less than the kernel buffers in 5 minutes. It's the same at `39998e2`. Fix: refresh the upstream timer instead of destroying when `upstream.isPaused()` or `socket.writableNeedDrain`, or use one tunnel-wide timer.
2. **A reset or idle cut on one side reaches the other as a clean FIN.** Plain `destroy()` at lines 277, 363, 364 and 369 never signals failure, so close-delimited streams read as complete. This is the remaining route to round 2's symptom. It's the same at `39998e2`. Fix: use `resetAndDestroy()` for failure and idle paths, keep `destroySoon()` for clean closes, and test that a mid-stream reset gives the PR `ECONNRESET`.
3. **Two teardown behaviours are untested:**
   - a host's clean close ending the PR side of a tunnel (mutants D2c and D2b survive);
   - a PR leaving mid-body freeing the plain-HTTP upstream (H5 survives).
