fix: an internal topology that falls back is never isolated, and the PR pane gets no DNS out

Round 2 of #40's review, and the items #39's round 3 carried over.

- A topology that can't be set up caps the check's result at unknown,
  saying egress isn't restricted, and is always logged (a new `log`
  option). The undo must remove the internal network, and refuses one
  still there, or the boot fails.
- On Docker, the PR pane's containers run with --dns 192.0.2.1, the
  workaround for CVE-2024-29018; Podman's get none.
- "Up" means read back: the fresh network is internal, and the forwarder
  runs and says it is ready within forwarderReadyMs. Triage adds its log
  tail.
- The podman-docker shim's failing `info` no longer sinks the verdict
  with the topology up.
- The proxy must answer the probe for every target, else unknown, naming
  the silent ones.
- The forwarder: idle means nothing moved through a tunnel or an answer,
  so backpressure from a slow but steady reader doesn't trip it, a
  stalled event loop gets a grace, and the timer's listener survives a
  re-arm. Resets rather than clean ends on the failure and idle paths;
  whitespace in forward.to and lookalike slashes refused.
- README: "If it can't be set up", DNS, readiness, cleanup and sweep.
