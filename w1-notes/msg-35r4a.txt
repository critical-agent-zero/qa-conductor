fix: the data engine's work is bounded by cells, flagging is linear, and what loses data is listed first

For #36's round 4, whose findings live in this engine:
- LIMITS.maxCells (500,000): a snapshot's cells (its rows' fields) are
  shared fairly like its bytes, counted before a row is read. Past its
  share a collection is cut short, so only its count is compared and it
  is flagged unverified. Masking, diffing and flagging take time in
  proportion to the cells, on the conductor's event loop.
- tally() merged each flag's items with a spread copy, quadratic in a
  field's rows: 10,000 rows with 100 fields cleared took 22 s to flag.
  It merges in place now.
- Within a severity and the base pane's collections, the kinds that lose
  data (delete, emptied, dropped, key-lost) come before column-dropped and
  mass-change, so 600 renamed columns can't push a delete out of a
  capped list.
