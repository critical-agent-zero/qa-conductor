fix: keys after a percent-escape and tags with a '<' in an attribute are masked again, and seeded dropped columns are named first

Round 5 of #35's review:
- A key right after a percent-escape counts (`x%3dpassword=`,
  `value%3a…--db-token=`, a regression of round 4's %3a and %3d), and a
  name that is a word is read past an escape's hex digits or letter
  (`%26pin%3D`, `\npw=`).
- A tag is read by its name, before whitespace, '/' or '>', and its
  attributes aren't scanned, so a '<' in one no longer hides a
  secret-named tag (a regression of round 4's `[^<>]`).
- column-dropped names first the dropped fields the base pane's before
  snapshot declares (`seeded`, their count), so padding columns the PR
  added can't hide a seeded one. Diff entries carry `fieldsBefore`.
- A base-pane collection the PR's snapshots may have left out has its
  unverified flag with what loses rows; that group is LOSES_ROWS now.
- The pair rule takes any name-like key, and a row shaped as a pair has
  its value fields masked. Go DSNs up to their last '@', any network and
  `@/`, never across a ':'; attribute values quoted either way or not; a
  flag before a tab; PGP keys.
- README: what masking still misses.
- Tests for each, the review's mutant cases, and the tag timing test's
  bound at 3 s.
