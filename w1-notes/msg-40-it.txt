test: the internal network on a real runtime, in CI on Docker Engine and Podman

A docker-integration job runs test/integration/docker-internal.test.mjs on
Linux, against Docker Engine and against Podman through its
Docker-compatible socket. It boots both panes with the internal topology
and checks:
- the probe says isolated, internal: true;
- from the PR pane: the host at every gateway, the base pane's database
  and 169.254.169.254 can't be reached, with a control from the base pane;
- an outside name doesn't resolve, and qa-egress does;
- the proxy refuses an unlisted host and metadata, and lets a listed
  address through;
- a forwarder that can't start falls back to unknown, and
  QA_REQUIRE_ISOLATION=1 refuses the session;
- teardown and sweep leave nothing.

The test runs only when QA_DOCKER_IT=1 asks: it creates and removes the
qa-* containers and networks. Asked, it fails rather than skips when no
runtime answers, and the job fails on any skip.
